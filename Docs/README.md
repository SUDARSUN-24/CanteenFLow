## Project Summary

CanteenFlow is a proposed campus food pre-order and pickup website designed to help university students avoid long ordering queues during short breaks. Students select food and a pickup time in advance, while vendors manage availability and prepare incoming orders in pickup-time order. Students collect and pay at the selected stall.

## MVP Summary

The MVP covers participating-stall and menu browsing, item availability, quantities, pickup-slot selection, validated order submission, unique order numbers, confirmation, student order-status viewing, and vendor controls for incoming orders, menu availability, status updates, and collection. It excludes online payment, delivery, ratings, loyalty programs, calorie filtering, AI recommendations, and multi-campus management.
